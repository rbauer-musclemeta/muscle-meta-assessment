16-Minute Walk TestAerobic Capacity
Submaximal cardiorespiratory fitness, aerobic capacity, and functional mobility.
Method:

  2. Patient walks as far as possible in 6 minutes at a safe, self-selected brisk pace.
  3. Resting breaks are permitted; the timer keeps running.
  4. Record total distance walked in meters.

230-Second Sit-to-StandLower Body Power
Quad/glute power, lower-body muscular endurance, and sarcopenia screening.
Method:
  1. Standard armless chair (seat height ~43 cm), placed against a wall.
  2. Patient sits mid-seat, feet flat, arms crossed over chest.
  3. On "Go," stand fully upright and sit back down as many times as possible in 30 seconds.
  4. Count only stands with full knee extension.

3Timed Up-and-GoAgility & Balance
Dynamic agility, gait speed, and fall-risk stratification.
Method:
  1. Patient begins seated in an armchair, back against the seat.
  2. Place a marker exactly 3 m (10 ft) away.
  3. On "Go," stand, walk to the marker at a comfortable pace, turn, walk back, and sit.
  4. Stop timing the instant the patient's back touches the seat. Lower time = better score.
